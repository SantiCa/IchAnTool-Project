# Paper 3 — Validation and palaeoenvironmental application

**Target:** Palaeogeography, Palaeoclimatology, Palaeoecology. Alternatives: Ichnos, Marine
Geology, Sedimentary Geology.

## Working title
Continuous, objective bioturbation records from core images: validation against visual
indices and application to [region/period] [TODO]

## Idea
- Validation: compare IchAnTool percentages with blind visual BI/ii estimates by several
  ichnologists on the same intervals; quantify subjectivity and bias of visual schemes.
- Application: continuous bioturbation and per-ichnotaxon records along long cores,
  correlated with geochemical/redox and SST proxies (e.g. Western Mediterranean during the
  Last Glacial Cycle; extends the ODP 977 example of Casanova-Arenillas et al., 2020).

## Outline
1. Introduction
2. Material — cores, images, age models, proxy data
3. Methods — IchAnTool workflow, observer experiment, statistics
4. Results — agreement and bias; continuous records
5. Discussion — what continuous records reveal that discrete estimates miss
6. Conclusions
